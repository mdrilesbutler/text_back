# text_back — leave-behind (owner one-pager)

## Never lose another after-hours call

You are on a job. The phone rings. They hang up and call the next company.

**text_back** texts them back in under a minute, asks what they need, and puts a **real appointment on your Google Calendar** — without changing the business number customers dial. Replies come from a dedicated text_back number that identifies your business.

### How it works
1. Customer calls your normal business number
2. If you miss it, we text: sorry we missed you — what were you calling about?
3. We collect job type, location, time, name
4. We book only when your calendar is free
5. You get the confirmation; they get the confirmation

### You keep control
- Keep your existing number (conditional forwarding)
- Deterministic STOP / HELP; quiet-hour enforcement is a Recover launch gate
- Nothing is “booked” without a real calendar event
- Human handoff when it is messy

### Simple math
Missed calls/week × close rate × job value = money leaking every month.
One recovered job often pays for the whole setup.
