Every missed call gets a reply within a minute

The unanswered call is forwarded to text_back and the caller gets one clear text asking what they need, before they have dialed anyone else. Wording is set by you and stays the same every time.

If they go quiet, a short follow-up sequence continues, never inside quiet hours, and stops the moment they respond.

Real availability

Books only the times you are actually free

It asks one question at a time to learn what the caller needs, where, and when, then offers only openings on your Google Calendar. A time is confirmed to the caller only after the calendar has created the event, so nothing gets double-booked.

A person when it matters

It handles the conversation. You can step in at any point.

It carries the whole exchange, not just the greeting. When a caller asks for a person, raises something urgent, or goes off-script, it pauses and sends you a short summary of what it has learned so far. Nothing is promised to the caller that you have not agreed to.
