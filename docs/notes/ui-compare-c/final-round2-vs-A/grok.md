1. One-tap section chips on the phone messages page, as on `Y/03-messages-phone-01.png` (Missed call, Questions, Booking, No reply). X’s “Jump to” is an extra tap. Make the chips sticky. Y’s chips disappear by `Y/03-messages-phone-02.png`.
2. The calendar empty sentence on `Y/05-calendar-phone-01.png`: “Bookings are not recorded on any calendar yet” and “Connecting a calendar from this page is still being set up by FluxWorks.” Keep X’s Check free times control and EXAMPLE labelling.
3. The segment-cost reason on `Y/03-messages-desktop-07.png` and `Y/03-messages-phone-07.png`: a special dash can force 70-character segments, next to “Billed as 3 texts.” Drop the unexplained red squares.

## 7. Recommendation

Portal X is the one an owner can run from a phone. Sign-in and sign-out, customer texts, appointment length, hours, quiet hours, reminders, calendar status, business and texting numbers, forwarding, and a setup check each have a findable page, with EXAMPLE labels, named save confirmations, and a code step before a mobile number changes. Portal Y’s messages, business name, and calendar status are clear, and its mobile-confirm page is solid, but schedule, forwarding, the business line, the texting number, setup status, and sign-out are missing or not shown, and the booking sample tells the owner a job is on their calendar while the calendar page says nothing is recorded. Before an owner sees X, lift the sticky save bar off the field it covers (`X/state-messages-unsaved-top-desktop.png`), replace “a test calendar you can't see” with Y’s plainer sentence, and take vendor plumbing such as “details go to FluxWorks” off the danger card (`X/04-messages-desktop-09.png`). The five phone tabs can stay. Account should be as easy to reach as Sign out already is from the header menu.
