## 6. Three ideas from Portal X that Portal Y should adopt

- Show the customer’s bad reply next to the reply it triggers. `X/03-messages-desktop-03.png` pairs CUSTOMER “Sometime next week” with the TextBack answer, and `X/03-messages-desktop-05.png` pairs “Wednesday at 10” with “That time is unavailable.” `Y/04-messages-desktop-03.png` and `Y/04-messages-phone-05.png` show only the outbound bubble, so the owner cannot see what caused it.
- Keep the vertical path of the conversation. `X/03-messages-desktop-01.png` uses a timed icon rail (missed call, questions, booking, clock) so a long script has a visible sequence. Y’s message pages are a denser stack of meta-plus-bubble rows.
- Mark the end of the thread. `X/03-messages-phone-08.png` and `X/03-messages-desktop-07.png` put a “Texts stop here” pill on the last reminder. `Y/04-messages-desktop-05.png` only says “After this the assistant stops texting” in the body.

## 7. Recommendation

Portal Y is the one an owner can run the business from. Home names the two steps still open, Schedule holds length, working hours, quiet hours, and reminder timing, Phone holds the business line, the texting number, the owner mobile, and carrier forwarding, Calendar says what Google will ask and labels the test slots, and Sign out is in the account menu with a confirm. Portal X edits the customer script more clearly, and its sign-in card names the business, but it has no hours, no forwarding, no way to connect a calendar, and no sign-out on screen, and the booking text it would send contains a private hostname and a past date. Before an owner uses Y, take the 1 AM warning off any page where hours are already set, stop calling EXAMPLE forwarding checks “real calls”, and say in owner words who receives a danger or step-back lead. In the same pass, unclip the phone jump chips, stop that sticky row from covering bubbles, and stop the phone unsaved bar from cutting off the second change.
