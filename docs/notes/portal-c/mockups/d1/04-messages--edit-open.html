  5. When the assistant steps back

    When a text needs a person, the assistant stops scheduling and passes the customer's details on.

    When the assistant steps back

    Sent when the customer asks for the owner or the assistant can't help.

    This text must tell the customer someone will follow up. Don't ask them to call, and don't say they'll be put through to someone.

    What happens next

    1. TextBack stops texting this customer.
    2. Their name, number and message go to FluxWorks.
    3. FluxWorks passes them to you. You follow up; the text promised the customer someone would.

    When the customer sends something like

    Customer

    Can I just talk to the owner?

    Oak & Pine Tree Care

    Got it. Automated scheduling is paused, and we'll follow up with you directly.
